Project Journey

From question to innovation.

Every project begins with curiosity. Students develop their ideas through research, experimentation, technology and collaboration.

01 — Ask

Start with a question, problem or idea worth exploring.

02 — Research

Investigate existing knowledge and develop a plan for testing the idea.

03 — Build

Create experiments, software, robots, websites, prototypes or digital experiences.

04 — Test

Test the project, analyze results and improve the design.

05 — Share

Present the final project to visitors and share what was discovered.
